# Returns and refunds

This document covers two different things: how merchants handle returns from their own customers, and Nestora's refund policy for its subscription fees.

## Customer returns (for merchants)

### Return window

Merchants choose their own return window in **Settings > Policies > Returns**, between 7 and 30 days after delivery. The default is **14 days**. Merchants can also mark specific products or collections as final sale (not returnable).

### Self-service returns

When self-service returns are turned on, customers start a return from their order page or the link in their delivery email. They choose the items and a reason. The merchant approves or rejects each request in **Orders > Returns**. Approved returns can generate a Nestora Ship return label, with the return shipping cost paid either by the merchant or deducted from the refund.

### Refunding customers

- Card and wallet payments made through NestPay are refunded to the original payment method. Refunds take 5 to 10 business days to appear, depending on the customer's bank.
- NestPay processing fees are **not** returned to the merchant when an order is refunded.
- COD orders are refunded by bank transfer, store credit or a gift card, which the merchant records in the refund dialog.
- Partial refunds are supported. The total refunded can never exceed the amount paid.

### Restocking

When a returned item is received, the merchant can tick **Restock** to add it back to inventory at the chosen location.

## Nestora subscription refunds

This section applies to fees that merchants pay to Nestora.

- **Monthly plans**: subscription fees are not refundable, except that a merchant can request a full refund of the **first paid month** within **7 days** of that first charge.
- **Annual plans**: a merchant can cancel within **30 days** of purchase or renewal and receive a prorated refund for the unused months. After 30 days annual fees are not refundable, but the store stays active until the end of the paid period.
- **Theme Store purchases**: refundable within 14 days if the theme has not been published.
- **Apps** from the App Marketplace are billed and refunded by each app developer under their own policy.
- **Domain purchases** are not refundable after registration.
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 10 business days.

To request a refund, contact support@nestora.example from the store owner's email address and include the store URL.

## Cancelling a store

The store owner can close the store from **Settings > Plan > Close store**. Closing a store stops future billing. The store's data is kept for **90 days** in case the merchant changes their mind, and is then permanently deleted. Download a full export from **Settings > Data export** before closing.
