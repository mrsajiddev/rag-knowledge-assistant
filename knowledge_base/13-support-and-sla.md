# Support and service levels

## Support channels by plan

| Plan | Channels | First response target |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | Email, Help Center | 24 hours |
| Growth | Email, live chat | 8 hours |
| Scale | Email, live chat, phone | 2 hours |
| Enterprise | All channels, 24/7, dedicated success manager | 30 minutes (15 minutes for P1) |

Response targets apply during support hours, except for Enterprise, which is covered 24/7.

## Support hours

Standard support runs **Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM Pakistan Standard Time (PKT)**, which is 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM in the UAE and 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM in Saudi Arabia. Support is closed on Sundays and on public holidays in Pakistan, except for P1 incidents, which are handled every day for Scale and Enterprise.

Support is available in English, Urdu and Arabic.

## Priority levels

- **P1 (critical)**: the storefront or checkout is down, or payments are failing for all customers.
- **P2 (high)**: a major feature is broken with no workaround, for example courier booking fails for all orders.
- **P3 (normal)**: a feature is partly broken or a workaround exists.
- **P4 (low)**: questions, how-to requests and feature requests.

## Uptime SLA

Scale and Enterprise plans include a monthly uptime commitment for the storefront and checkout:

- Scale: **99.9%** monthly uptime
- Enterprise: **99.95%** monthly uptime

If uptime falls below the commitment, the merchant receives a service credit on the next invoice:

| Monthly uptime | Credit |
|---|---|
| Below the SLA but at least 99.0% | 10% of the monthly plan fee |
| Below 99.0% but at least 95.0% | 25% of the monthly plan fee |
| Below 95.0% | 50% of the monthly plan fee |

Credits must be requested within **30 days** of the end of the affected month by contacting support. Scheduled maintenance announced at least 48 hours in advance, and problems caused by third-party apps or the merchant's own DNS, do not count as downtime. Starter and Growth plans have no financial SLA, although the same infrastructure is used for all plans.

## Status page

Live system status and incident history are published at https://status.nestora.example. Merchants can subscribe to updates by email or SMS.

## Scheduled maintenance

Planned maintenance happens on Sundays between 2:00 AM and 5:00 AM PKT, is announced at least 48 hours ahead on the status page, and rarely affects the storefront.

## Help Center and community

The Help Center at https://help.nestora.example has step-by-step guides and videos in English and Urdu. The Nestora Community forum is where merchants and app developers share tips; Nestora staff answer developer questions there within two business days.

## Nestora Experts

For design, migration or custom development work, merchants can hire a certified partner from the Nestora Experts directory. Experts are independent agencies and freelancers who have passed Nestora's certification exam.
