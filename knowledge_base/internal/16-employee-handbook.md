# Employee handbook

This handbook is for Nestora employees. It summarises the most-asked-about people policies. The full policies are in the HR portal ("Haven").

## Working hours and hybrid work

The standard working week is Monday to Friday, 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM local time, with a one-hour lunch break. Core collaboration hours, when everyone should be reachable, are **11:00 AM to 4:00 PM**.

Nestora works in a hybrid model: employees in Lahore, Karachi and Dubai work from the office **three days a week, Tuesday to Thursday**. Mondays and Fridays are flexible. Fully remote roles are agreed in the offer letter. Employees may work from another city for up to 4 weeks a year with their manager's approval.

During Ramadan, office hours are 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM.

## Leave

| Leave type | Allowance |
|---|---|
| Annual leave | 20 working days per year |
| Sick leave | 10 working days per year |
| Casual leave | 5 working days per year |
| Maternity leave | 16 weeks fully paid |
| Paternity leave | 10 working days fully paid |
| Bereavement leave | 5 working days |
| Hajj / Umrah leave | 15 working days, once during employment |

Annual leave accrues monthly. Up to 5 unused annual-leave days can be carried into the next year and must be used by 31 March. Sick leave of more than 2 consecutive days needs a medical certificate. Leave is requested in Haven and approved by the line manager; requests of more than 5 days should be made at least 2 weeks ahead.

Public holidays follow the official calendar of the country where the employee is based.

## Probation and notice

New employees have a **3-month probation period**. During probation either side can end employment with 2 weeks' notice. After probation the notice period is **1 month**, or **2 months** for employees at Senior level and above.

## Pay and benefits

- Salaries are paid on the **last working day of each month**.
- An annual performance review happens in March, and salary changes take effect in April.
- **Health insurance** covers the employee, spouse, children and parents, with inpatient cover of up to PKR 1,500,000 per family per year and outpatient cover of PKR 100,000 per year. Dubai-based employees have equivalent cover under a UAE plan.
- **Provident fund**: Nestora matches employee contributions of up to 8.33% of basic salary.
- **Learning budget**: PKR 150,000 per year (or the equivalent in AED) for courses, books, certifications and conferences, approved by the manager.
- **Equipment**: every employee receives a laptop (engineers and designers get a MacBook Pro), plus a one-time home-office allowance of PKR 60,000.
- **Internet allowance**: PKR 5,000 per month.
- **Employee referral bonus**: PKR 200,000 for a successful engineering hire and PKR 100,000 for other roles, paid after the new hire completes probation.

## Expenses

Business expenses (travel, client meals, software approved by IT) are claimed in Haven with a receipt photo within **30 days**. Claims above PKR 50,000 need approval from a department head. Domestic air travel is economy class; international flights over 6 hours may be premium economy.

## Code of conduct

Nestora expects respectful, honest behaviour toward colleagues, merchants and partners. Harassment and discrimination of any kind are not tolerated. Concerns can be raised with a manager, with HR at people@nestora.example, or anonymously through the Speak Up form in Haven. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is a serious disciplinary offence.

## Who to contact

- HR and leave questions: people@nestora.example
- IT help (laptops, accounts, access): it@nestora.example, or #it-help on Slack
- Payroll: payroll@nestora.example
- Office facilities: the office manager of each location
