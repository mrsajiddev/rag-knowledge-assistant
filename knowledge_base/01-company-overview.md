# Company overview

## Who we are

Nestora Commerce is an e-commerce platform that lets small and medium-sized businesses open an online store, take payments, ship orders and manage customers from one dashboard. The company was founded in 2019 in Lahore, Pakistan, by Ayesha Malik (Chief Executive Officer) and Bilal Qureshi (Chief Technology Officer). Before Nestora, Ayesha ran an online fashion label and Bilal led the payments engineering team at a regional bank.

Nestora's headquarters are in Gulberg III, Lahore. The company also has offices in Karachi (Shahrah-e-Faisal) and Dubai (Dubai Internet City). As of October 2026 Nestora employs around 180 people and serves more than 14,000 active merchants in Pakistan, the United Arab Emirates and Saudi Arabia.

## Mission

Our mission is to make it as easy for a home-based seller in Multan to run a professional online store as it is for a national brand. We focus on the problems that matter most in our markets: cash on delivery, local couriers, mobile wallets, Urdu and Arabic storefronts, and support in local time zones.

## Products

- **Nestora Stores**: the online store builder, with themes, a drag-and-drop page editor, product catalogue, discounts and customer accounts.
- **NestPay**: Nestora's built-in payment processing for cards, bank transfers and mobile wallets, with payouts to local bank accounts.
- **Nestora Ship**: shipping labels, courier booking and cash-on-delivery reconciliation with partner couriers.
- **Nestora POS**: a point-of-sale app for Android tablets that syncs inventory between physical shops and the online store.
- **Nestora Developers**: the REST API, webhooks, official SDKs and the App Marketplace.

## Funding and milestones

- 2019: Founded in Lahore; first 50 merchants onboarded by hand.
- 2020: Launched cash-on-delivery verification during the surge in online shopping.
- 2021: Raised a USD 4 million seed round; launched NestPay.
- 2023: Raised a USD 18 million Series A; opened the Dubai office and entered the UAE.
- 2024: Launched Nestora POS and expanded to Saudi Arabia.
- 2026: Released API v2, Urdu (right-to-left) storefronts and B2B wholesale price lists.
