# Getting started with your store

This guide takes a new merchant from sign-up to the first order. Most merchants launch within two days.

## 1. Create your account

Sign up at https://www.nestora.example/start with an email address or a Google account. Choose your store name and country. The store name can be changed later; the country sets your default currency and tax settings and can only be changed by support.

Two-factor authentication (2FA) is mandatory for store owners. Set it up during sign-up with an authenticator app or SMS.

## 2. Pick and customise a theme

Go to **Online Store > Themes**. Nestora includes 12 free themes. Premium themes from the Theme Store cost between $49 and $180 as a one-time purchase. Every theme supports right-to-left layouts for Urdu and Arabic storefronts.

Use the page editor to change colours, fonts, the logo, the home-page sections and the navigation menus. Changes are saved as a draft until you press **Publish**.

## 3. Add products

Go to **Products > Add product** to add items one by one, or use **Products > Import** to upload a CSV file. See the Products and inventory guide for CSV columns and variant limits.

## 4. Set up payments

Go to **Settings > Payments**.

- **NestPay** is activated automatically. To receive payouts you must verify your business: upload your CNIC (or Emirates ID / Iqama) and add a bank account in your own or your business's name. Verification usually takes one to two business days.
- **Cash on delivery** is turned on by default for Pakistan stores. You can set a minimum and maximum order value for COD.
- External gateways can be connected from the App Marketplace, subject to the platform transaction fee of your plan.

## 5. Set up shipping

Go to **Settings > Shipping**. Add your pickup address, create shipping zones (for example "Lahore", "Rest of Punjab", "Rest of Pakistan") and set flat or weight-based rates. To book couriers from the dashboard, connect Nestora Ship.

## 6. Connect a domain

Every store gets a free address such as `yourstore.nestora.shop`. To use your own domain, go to **Settings > Domains > Connect domain** and add these DNS records at your domain registrar:

- An `A` record for `@` pointing to `203.0.113.40`
- A `CNAME` record for `www` pointing to `stores.nestora.example`

DNS changes can take up to 48 hours to propagate. The SSL certificate is issued automatically once DNS is verified, which usually takes under an hour but can take up to 24 hours. You can also buy a domain directly in Nestora from $14 per year.

## 7. Pre-launch checklist

- Place a test order using NestPay test mode (**Settings > Payments > Test mode**).
- Add your refund and return policy, privacy policy and terms (templates are in **Settings > Policies**).
- Add a contact page and your business address.
- Check the store on a mobile phone; more than 80% of Nestora storefront traffic is mobile.
- Remove the storefront password in **Online Store > Preferences**.

## 8. Launch

Choose a plan in **Settings > Plan** before your trial ends, remove the storefront password, and share your store link. The **Home** dashboard shows your first sales, visitors and conversion rate in real time.
