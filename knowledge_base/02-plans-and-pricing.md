# Plans and pricing

Nestora has four plans: Starter, Growth, Scale and Enterprise. All plans include hosting, SSL certificates, a custom domain connection, unlimited bandwidth and the online store builder. Prices below are per store, per month, excluding taxes.

## Plan prices

| Plan | Monthly (USD) | Monthly (PKR) | Billed annually (USD/month) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | $19 | PKR 5,400 | $15.20 |
| Growth | $49 | PKR 13,900 | $39.20 |
| Scale | $149 | PKR 41,900 | $119.20 |
| Enterprise | from $900 | custom quote | custom |

Annual billing gives a 20% discount on every self-serve plan. Merchants in Pakistan are billed in PKR; merchants in the UAE and Saudi Arabia are billed in USD. Sales tax is added according to the merchant's billing address.

## Plan limits

| Limit | Starter | Growth | Scale | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Products | 500 | 10,000 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Staff accounts | 2 | 10 | 50 | Unlimited |
| Inventory locations | 1 | 2 | 10 | Unlimited |
| Media storage | 5 GB | 50 GB | 500 GB | Custom |
| API rate limit | 2 requests/s | 5 requests/s | 20 requests/s | 50 requests/s |

API limits allow short bursts of up to twice the per-second rate.

## Transaction fees

If a store uses NestPay, Nestora charges no extra transaction fee; only the NestPay processing rate applies. If a store uses an external payment gateway, Nestora adds a platform transaction fee on each order:

- Starter: 2.0%
- Growth: 1.0%
- Scale: 0.5%
- Enterprise: 0%

Cash-on-delivery orders never carry a platform transaction fee.

## NestPay card processing rates

- Starter: 3.2% + PKR 15 per successful card payment
- Growth: 2.9% per successful card payment
- Scale: 2.6% per successful card payment
- Enterprise: negotiated rates
- Mobile wallet payments: 1.8% on every plan
- Bank transfer (IBFT): flat PKR 25 per payment on every plan

## Features by plan

- **Starter**: online store, discount codes, cash on delivery, NestPay, Nestora Ship, email support.
- **Growth**: everything in Starter, plus abandoned-cart recovery emails and WhatsApp messages, gift cards, removal of the "Powered by Nestora" footer, live chat support, and the professional reports pack.
- **Scale**: everything in Growth, plus B2B wholesale price lists, advanced reports with scheduled exports, up to 10 inventory locations, phone support and a 99.9% uptime SLA.
- **Enterprise**: everything in Scale, plus single sign-on (SAML), a dedicated customer success manager, 24/7 priority support, a 99.95% uptime SLA, custom contracts and invoicing.

## Free trial

Every new store starts with a 14-day free trial of the Growth plan. No credit card is needed to start. Support can extend a trial once by 7 days. At the end of the trial the store is paused until the merchant chooses a plan; nothing is deleted for 90 days.

## Changing plans

Upgrades take effect immediately and the difference is prorated on the next invoice. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle. If a store exceeds a limit of the lower plan (for example, more than 500 products when moving to Starter), the downgrade is blocked until the store is within the limit.

## Nestora POS pricing

Nestora POS costs $29 per location per month (PKR 8,200) on any plan. The first location is included free on Scale and Enterprise.
