# Frequently asked questions

## Can I sell on Nestora without a registered company?

Yes. Individuals can sell on Nestora and receive NestPay payouts with a CNIC (Pakistan), Emirates ID (UAE) or Iqama/national ID (Saudi Arabia) and a bank account in their own name. A registered business is required for the Enterprise plan and for B2B wholesale invoicing with an NTN or TRN.

## Do I need technical skills?

No. The store builder, themes and dashboard work without code. Developers can still edit theme code (Liquid-style templates, CSS and JavaScript) in **Online Store > Themes > Edit code**.

## Can I move my store from WooCommerce or Shopify?

From WooCommerce, use the free WooCommerce importer app, which copies products, customers and order history. From other platforms, export products and customers as CSV and use **Products > Import** and **Customers > Import**. Certified Nestora Experts can handle the migration for you.

## Is there a setup fee or long contract?

No. There is no setup fee and plans are month-to-month unless you choose annual billing. Enterprise contracts are normally for 12 months.

## Can I have more than one store?

Yes. Each store needs its own plan. Owners can switch between stores from the store menu. Multi-store organisations on Enterprise get consolidated billing.

## How do I give my accountant access?

Add them as a staff member in **Settings > Users** and give only the **Reports** and **Payouts (view)** permissions. Staff accounts count toward your plan limit (2 on Starter, 10 on Growth, 50 on Scale).

## Why was my COD order cancelled automatically?

The customer did not confirm the order through the WhatsApp or SMS verification message within 48 hours. You can turn off COD verification in **Settings > Payments > Cash on delivery**, but this usually increases refused parcels.

## How long do payouts take?

Daily, two business days after capture (T+2) on Growth, Scale and Enterprise. Weekly on Tuesdays on Starter. A new merchant's first payout is held for 7 days. COD money collected by Nestora Ship couriers is added to your balance within 5 business days after delivery.

## Can I use my own domain?

Yes, on every plan, including Starter. Point an `A` record to `203.0.113.40` and a `www` `CNAME` to `stores.nestora.example`, then connect the domain in **Settings > Domains**.

## Does Nestora support Urdu and Arabic storefronts?

Yes. Since release 6.1 all free themes fully support right-to-left Urdu storefronts, and Arabic has been supported since release 5.0. A store can offer up to 3 languages, and customers switch languages from the store header.

## Can customers pay in instalments?

Not yet through NestPay. Some apps in the App Marketplace offer buy-now-pay-later with partner lenders; availability depends on the country.

## What happens to my store if I stop paying?

If a payment fails, Nestora retries the charge 3 times over 9 days and emails the owner each time. After that the store is paused: the storefront shows a "store unavailable" page, but the dashboard and data remain. Data is deleted 90 days after the store is paused unless a plan is chosen.

## Is there a mobile app for merchants?

Merchants can manage orders from the mobile-friendly dashboard and use Nestora POS on Android tablets. A native iOS app for merchants is on the roadmap.
